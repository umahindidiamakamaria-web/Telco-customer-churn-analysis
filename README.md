## Key Findings

 **Contract type is the strongest churn driver**: Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7%, compared to 11.3% for one-year and just 2.8% for two-year contracts — a ~15x gap between the extremes.

 **The first year is the highest-risk period**: Churn peaks at 47.4% within the first 12 months and steadily declines to 6.6% by the 61-72 month mark, showing loyalty builds significantly over time.

 **Electronic check payments show disproportionate churn**: 45.3% of electronic check users churn, nearly 3x higher than automatic payment methods (15-17%) — and it's the largest payment segment in the customer base.

 **Fiber optic customers churn more than any other service tier**: 41.9%, more than double DSL customers (19.0%), despite being the premium offering.

 **Tenure is the strongest numerical correlate of churn** (correlation of -0.35), confirmed through correlation analysis across key account metrics.

 **Service adoption shows a non-linear churn pattern**: churn peaks among customers with just 1 add-on service (45.8%) before dropping steadily as service count increases, reaching 5.3% for customers with all 6 services.
